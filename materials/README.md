## Further reading

- H. Haug and A.-P. Jauho, *Quantum Kinetics in Transport and Optics of Semiconductors*, Springer Series in Solid-State Sciences **123**. This is the textbook that the Keldysh lecture notes summarise.
- A. Kamenev, *Many-body theory of non-equilibrium systems*, [arXiv:cond-mat/0412296](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0412296). These are free lecture notes on the Keldysh field theory.
- H. Bruus and K. Flensberg, *Many-Body Quantum Theory in Condensed Matter Physics*, Oxford University Press. It covers the equilibrium Green's functions, Wick's theorem and Feynman diagrams that topic 01 assumes.

### For topic 04

- B. Mera and T. Ozawa, *Kähler geometry and Chern insulators: Relations between topology and the quantum metric*, Phys. Rev. B **104**, 045104 (2021), [arXiv:2103.11583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11583); and T. Ozawa and B. Mera, Phys. Rev. B **104**, 045103 (2021), [arXiv:2103.11582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11582). The pullback picture: quantum metric and Berry curvature as the Riemannian metric and symplectic form of the space of states.
- A. Avdoshkin and F. K. Popov, *Extrinsic geometry of quantum states*, Phys. Rev. B **107**, 245136 (2023), [arXiv:2205.15353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15353). Bargmann invariants, extrinsic geometry, Gauss–Codazzi equations for the state manifold (Appendix D).
- A. Avdoshkin, *Geometry of degenerate quantum states, configurations of m-planes and invariants on complex Grassmannians*, [arXiv:2404.03234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03234). Principal angles and invariants for degenerate states.
- T. Bendokat, R. Zimmermann, P.-A. Absil, *A Grassmann manifold handbook: basic geometry and computational aspects*, [arXiv:2011.13699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13699). Geodesics, principal angles and numerics on the Grassmannian.
- H. F. Lai, *Characteristic classes of real manifolds immersed in complex manifolds*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **172**, 1–33 (1972), [doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-1972-0314066-8](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1972-0314066-8); F. Forstnerič, *Stein domains in complex surfaces*, [arXiv:math/0201097](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0201097). Complex points of real surfaces in complex surfaces.
- J. R. Quine, *A global theorem for singularities of maps between oriented 2-manifolds*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **236**, 307–314 (1978). Folds, cusps and the degree of a surface map.
- K. Saji, M. Umehara, K. Yamada, *The geometry of fronts*, Ann. of Math. **169**, 491–529 (2009). Singular curvature and Gauss–Bonnet for fronts, used in H3.
